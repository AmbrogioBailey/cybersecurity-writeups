## What Is Defensive Security?

Defensive security is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and websites from attacks — with the goal of stopping threats before they cause damage.

It differs from **offensive security**, which involves actively simulating the techniques an attacker would use in order to find and close vulnerabilities before a real attack happens. Defensive security, by contrast, focuses on detecting and responding to attacks that are already underway or actively targeting a system.

The core principle: **detect threats early and respond before any real damage occurs.**

---

## What I Learned

In this room, I navigated a simulated Security Operations Center (SOC) environment to identify and stop an active intrusion attempt.

The scenario involved a hacker attempting to gain unauthorized access to an admin's bank account. My objective was to:

1. Analyze the simulated security dashboard to identify suspicious activity
2. Locate the attacker's IP address from the alert data
3. Enter the IP address into the system and **block** it to cut off the attack before any damage was done

This hands-on exercise reinforced how real SOC analysts work — monitoring live traffic, triaging alerts, and making quick decisions to neutralize threats in real time.

---

## Key Takeaway

Defensive security isn't passive. It requires active monitoring, fast analysis, and decisive action. The ability to identify *who* is attacking, *what* they're targeting, and *how* to stop them — before damage occurs — is the foundation of any effective security posture.
